# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want JeDavid AI Skills to be a practical, welcoming, technically rigorous open-source project.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and technical disagreements respectfully;
- critique implementations and evidence, not people;
- welcome contributors regardless of experience level or background;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior;
- respect privacy and never publish credentials, customer data, or sensitive infrastructure information;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when recommending vendors, products, or services.

## Technical discussions

Strong technical disagreement is welcome. Prefer reproducible evidence, primary documentation, benchmarks, tests, and concrete examples over authority or rhetoric.

Security-sensitive discussions must follow `SECURITY.md`. Do not publish exploitable private-system details in public issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact information available on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and is informed by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant principles.
